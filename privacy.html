Privacy Policy

How DC Overwatcher may process and retain data.

HomeTerms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

1. Overview

This Policy describes how the operator of DC Overwatcher (“Bot”) may process information when the Bot is used in a Discord server. Exact data depends on enabled features and the deployed version.

2. Types of information that may be processed

  • Discord identifiers: user, server, channel, role, and message identifiers needed for features.
  • Moderation records: warnings, case numbers, staff actions, timeout records, and related reasons where used.
  • Ticket data: ticket status, opener/assigned staff identifiers, messages and attachment links referenced in transcripts, where enabled.
  • Configuration: server settings such as log channels, support roles, automod thresholds, and welcome/ticket settings.
  • Activity data: XP, levels, or reward records if leveling features are enabled.
  • Message/event content: content needed for enabled filters, logging, or ticket transcripts. The Bot should not be assumed to collect every message in every channel; access depends on code, permissions, intents, and configuration.

3. Why information is used

Information may be used to deliver moderation, ticket support, logging, welcome/member events, leveling, security, troubleshooting, and configuration persistence; enforce server rules; and respond to support or deletion requests.

4. Storage and sharing

In the standard project configuration, persistent records may be stored in a local SQLite database on the machine or hosting environment running the Bot. The operator may also rely on Discord and the selected hosting provider. Data is not intentionally sold. Data may be disclosed if required by law or necessary to protect security, or when explicitly directed by the relevant user or server administrator where lawful.

5. Retention

Records may be retained while needed to provide features, maintain moderation history, or troubleshoot. Retention depends on deployed code and server settings. Removing the Bot does not necessarily delete database records or backups automatically; the operator should delete records when no longer needed or when a valid deletion request applies.

6. Choices and deletion requests

For requests to access, correct, or delete data handled by the Bot, contact the operator through the DC Overwatcher community Discord. Include relevant server/record details, but never post passwords or tokens. Requests will be reviewed and handled as required by applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or moderation reasons.

7. Security

The operator should restrict database and hosting access, keep bot tokens secret, apply updates, and limit Discord permissions. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

8. Children

Users must meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and any higher age requirements applicable in their location.

9. Changes and contact

This Policy may be updated as features, hosting, or legal requirements change. For privacy questions or requests, contact the operator through the DC Overwatcher community Discord.

Operator review required: Verify the actual hosting setup, database location, logs/transcripts, backups, retention settings, and third-party services. Update anything that does not match the deployed Bot.
